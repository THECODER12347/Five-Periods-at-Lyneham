Controls/Instructions

To reset:
* Camera System: Press C
* Audio System: Press A
* Ventilation System: Press V
If the game seems stuck on the disclaimers, just press any button (space is a good option) and it will continue after a few seconds.
For the tasks, type after the text bubble has disappeared. Remember that every key is detected, though there may not be much shown, so be cautious.

Jump to Perfect alignment for game.
